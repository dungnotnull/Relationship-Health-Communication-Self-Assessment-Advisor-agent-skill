# SECOND-BRAIN-KNOWLEDGE-PAPER.md — Relationship Health & Communication Self-Assessment Advisor

A curated knowledge base of research papers, books, and authoritative sources underpinning this skill's methodology. These should be consulted (and their principles extracted into skill reference files) when building the actual skill.

> **Note on sourcing:** This list was compiled from general subject-matter knowledge to give the skill a strong research foundation to build from. Before relying on any specific citation (e.g., in an academic, legal, or professional deliverable), verify the exact title, year, and venue independently, as this list has not been individually re-verified against live databases at compilation time.

## Research Papers & Sources (20)

1. J. Gottman, N. Silver (1999). The Seven Principles for Making Marriage Work. Crown — Foundational Sound Relationship House framework.
2. J. Gottman (1994). What Predicts Divorce? The Relationship Between Marital Processes and Marital Outcomes. Erlbaum — Origin of the Four Horsemen empirical framework.
3. J. Gottman, R. Levenson (2000). The Timing of Divorce: Predicting When a Couple Will Divorce over a 14-Year Period. Journal of Marriage and Family — Longitudinal population-level (not individual-diagnostic) predictor research.
4. J. Bowlby (1969). Attachment and Loss, Vol. 1: Attachment. Basic Books — Foundational attachment theory.
5. M. Ainsworth (1978). Patterns of Attachment: A Psychological Study of the Strange Situation. Erlbaum — Empirical attachment-style classification research.
6. C. Rusbult (1980). Commitment and Satisfaction in Romantic Associations: A Test of the Investment Model. Journal of Experimental Social Psychology — Foundational Investment Model of Commitment.
7. S. Gable, H. Reis, E. Impett, E. Asher (2004). What Do You Do When Things Go Right? The Intrapersonal and Interpersonal Benefits of Sharing Positive Events. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology — Origin of active-constructive responding research.
8. J. Gottman, J. Schwartz Gottman (2008). Gottman Method Couple Therapy. In Clinical Handbook of Couple Therapy — Applied clinical framework for relationship-health interventions.
9. P. Amato (2010). Research on Divorce: Continuing Trends and New Developments. Journal of Marriage and Family — Population-level (not individual) divorce-risk-factor research, used only for general education.
10. B. Fincham, S. Beach (2010). Of Memes and Marriage: Toward a Positive Relationship Science. Journal of Family Theory & Review — Positive-psychology framing for relationship-health research.
11. E. Finkel, C. Hui, K. Carswell, G. Larson (2014). The Suffocation of Marriage: Climbing Mount Maslow Without Enough Oxygen. Psychological Inquiry — Modern relationship-science framework on evolving marital expectations.
12. S. Sprecher, D. Felmlee (2000). Romantic Partners' Perceptions of Social Network Attributes with the Passage of Time and Relationship Transitions. Personal Relationships — Social-context research relevant to relationship health (population-level).
13. R. Levenson, L. Carstensen, J. Gottman (1993). Long-Term Marriage: Age, Gender, and Satisfaction. Psychology and Aging — Longitudinal satisfaction research.
14. T. Huston, S. Niehuis, S. Smith (2001). The Early Marital Roots of Conjugal Distress and Divorce. Current Directions in Psychological Science — Early-relationship predictor research (population-level education only).
15. J. Karney, T. Bradbury (1995). The Longitudinal Course of Marital Quality and Stability: A Review of Theory, Methods, and Research. Psychological Bulletin — Comprehensive review of marital-quality research methodology.
16. S. Johnson (2004). The Practice of Emotionally Focused Couple Therapy: Creating Connection (2nd ed.). Brunner-Routledge — Emotionally Focused Therapy framework for couples counseling referral context.
17. H. Markman, S. Stanley, S. Blumberg (2010). Fighting for Your Marriage. Jossey-Bass — Applied evidence-based communication-skills program for couples.
18. E. Berscheid, H. Reis (1998). Attraction and Close Relationships. In The Handbook of Social Psychology — Foundational relationship-science reference.
19. B. Le, C. Agnew (2003). Commitment and Its Theorized Determinants: A Meta-Analysis of the Investment Model. Personal Relationships — Meta-analytic validation of the Investment Model.
20. American Association for Marriage and Family Therapy (2020). Marriage and Couples Counseling: Effectiveness Research Summary. AAMFT — Evidence base supporting the professional-referral pathway for serious relationship distress.
