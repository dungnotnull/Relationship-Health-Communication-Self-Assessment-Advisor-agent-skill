# Sound Relationship House Self-Check — Assessment Template

> Mutual use: each partner completes separately, then compares. Not an individual diagnostic; not a divorce forecast.

## Instrument

7 items, 1–5 scale (1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree). Higher is healthier.

| ID | Domain | Prompt |
|----|--------|-------|
| srh1 | love-maps | I can describe my partner’s inner world, stresses, joys, and current dreams. |
| srh2 | fondness-admiration | I express fondness and admiration for my partner regularly. |
| srh3 | turning-toward | I turn toward my partner’s small bids for connection. |
| srh4 | conflict-management (gentle startup) | In a conflict, I start conversations gently rather than with criticism. |
| srh5 | repair | We repair effectively when conversations get off track. |
| srh6 | dreams | We support each other’s life dreams and roles. |
| srh7 | shared-meaning | We share rituals of connection and a sense of shared meaning/values. |

## Scoring (raw sum 7–35)

| Raw | Band | Interpretation |
|-----|------|----------------|
| 7–19 | Needs attention | Several levels underdeveloped; friendship and/or conflict management need strengthening. |
| 20–27 | Developing | Workable foundation with specific gaps. |
| 28–35 | Strong | Robust Sound Relationship House. |

## Suggested actions

- **Needs attention:** strengthen friendship first (love maps, fondness/admiration, turning toward). Counselor if distress high.
- **Developing:** target the lowest-scoring level with one exercise.
- **Strong:** maintain with regular rituals of connection.

## Reliable change (re-administration)

Approximate RCI threshold: 4 points. Crossing from a lower band into “Strong” = clinically significant change.

## Source
Gottman, J. M., & Silver, N. (1999). *The Seven Principles for Making Marriage Work*.
