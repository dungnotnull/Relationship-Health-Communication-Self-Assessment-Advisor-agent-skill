# Professional Referral Guidance

> **When in doubt, refer.** This skill educates and reflects; it does not treat.

## When to recommend professional help

- Persistent, painful conflict despite the couple’s own efforts.
- Betrayal, affair, or broken trust.
- The same fight recurring with no repair.
- Frequent contempt the couple cannot reduce alone.
- Chase–withdraw (pursuer–distancer) cycles that do not de-escalate.
- Any sign of disorganized / trauma-linked attachment.
- Intimate-partner violence — refer to a hotline **before** joint counseling. Couples counseling is contraindicated where ongoing abuse is present.

## Types of qualified professionals

- **Marriage and Family Therapists (MFT / LMFT)** — licensed clinicians specializing in couples and families.
- **EFT-trained couples therapists** — Emotionally Focused Therapy; targets negative cycles and attachment needs; recovery from distress ~70–75%.
- **Gottman Method-trained therapists** — structured assessment + skills + shared meaning.
- **PREP / CPREP-trained counselors** — communication-skills focused prevention and enrichment.
- **Faith-integrated counselors** — if requested; clarify that a licensed clinician is still recommended for clinical distress.

## How to find one

- Primary-care provider referral.
- Insurer’s in-network directory.
- Professional-association locators (e.g., AAMFT therapist locator in the US; BACP / COSRT in the UK).
- Culturally-matching services: bilingual/bicultural clinicians, community clinics, sliding-scale clinics.
- Online options are legitimate; note cross-jurisdiction licensure limits for therapists.

## Crisis / violence resources (surfaced immediately on detection)

- **US National Domestic Violence Hotline:** 1-800-799-7233 or text START to 88788 (24/7, confidential).
- **Emergency services:** 911 (US/Canada), 112 (EU), 999 (UK), 113 (Vietnam police).
- **International directory:** https://www.hotpeachpages.org/

## Referral phrasing template

```
For [describe concern], a qualified couples counselor or marriage and family
therapist can help. EFT and Gottman Method are well-supported approaches. You can
find a licensed clinician through [options]. If you feel unsafe or afraid of your
partner, contact a domestic-violence hotline before joint counseling.
```
