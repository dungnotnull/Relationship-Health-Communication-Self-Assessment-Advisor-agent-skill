# Gottman Sound Relationship House — Operational Reference

> **Scope reminder:** population-level relationship-science research. Cannot diagnose an individual couple or predict whether a specific partner is unfaithful or will leave. Use only for mutual self-reflection.

## Source
- Gottman, J. M., & Silver, N. (1999). *The Seven Principles for Making Marriage Work*. Crown.
- Gottman, J. M., & Schwartz Gottman, J. (2008). Gottman Method Couple Therapy. In *Clinical Handbook of Couple Therapy*. Guilford.
- Levenson, R. W., Carstensen, L. L., & Gottman, J. M. (1993). Long-Term Marriage. *Psychology and Aging*.

## The Seven Levels (operationalized)

1. **Build Love Maps** — know your partner’s inner psychological world: worries, joys, stresses, current dreams, important people, values.
2. **Share Fondness and Admiration** — a culture of appreciation; 5:1 positive-to-negative interaction ratio in stable couples.
3. **Turn Toward Instead of Away** — notice and respond to small “bids for connection” (questions, gestures, stories).
4. **The Positive Perspective** — default charitable interpretation of partner’s behavior.
5. **Manage Conflict** — gentle startup, accept influence, soothe, compromise, tolerate physiological flooding (break if >~100 bpm).
6. **Make Life Dreams Come True** — support each other’s aspirations and roles.
7. **Create Shared Meaning** — rituals, roles, goals, values that give the relationship a sense of purpose.

## Concrete operational principle (distilled, not just cited)

**Friendship is the foundation.** Levels 1–3 (love maps, fondness/admiration, turning toward) form the friendship basis that makes conflict manageable. Couples in distress almost always have a weakened friendship layer first. Strengthening these three has the largest leverage before any conflict-management work.

## How the skill applies this

- The **Sound Relationship House Self-Check** (`references/assessments/sound-relationship-house-check.md`) maps one item to each level.
- Scoring bands surface the weakest level and recommend a targeted exercise from `references/prompts/`.
- The orchestrator never produces a “divorce risk” number for an individual couple.

## Effect sizes / evidence quality

- Population-level divorce-prediction accuracy ~0.85 across observational studies (not individual).
- Gottman Method marital satisfaction pre→post *d* ≈ 0.72.
- Evidence grade: **A** (strong), with the explicit caveat that predictors are population-level.

## Limitations
- Original samples predominantly white, middle-class US couples.
- Prediction is statistical, not idiographic; do not apply to a single couple as a forecast.

## Referral trigger
If a couple scores in the “Needs attention” band and reports high distress or frequent contempt, recommend a Gottman Method-trained or EFT-trained couples therapist.
