# The Four Horsemen — Operational Reference

> **Scope reminder:** patterns to address together, not labels to assign to a partner. Population-level associations only.

## Source
- Gottman, J. M. (1994). *What Predicts Divorce?* Erlbaum.
- Gottman, J. M., & Schwartz Gottman, J. (2008). Gottman Method Couple Therapy.

## The Four Horsemen

1. **Criticism** — attacking a partner’s character (“You never think of anyone but yourself”) rather than raising a complaint about behavior.
   - **Antidote: Gentle Startup.** Formula: “I feel [emotion] about [specific situation] because [reason]; I need [specific, positive request].” Avoid “you always / you never.”
2. **Contempt** — superiority: sarcasm, eye-rolling, name-calling, mocking. The strongest population-level predictor of deterioration.
   - **Antidote: Culture of Appreciation.** Daily, specific appreciation; describe own feelings/needs rather than partner’s flaws; shared rituals of respect.
3. **Defensiveness** — counter-attack, victim-playing, denying responsibility (“It’s not my fault, you’re the one who…”).
   - **Antidote: Take Responsibility** — own even a piece of the problem; “You’re right, I did X. Let’s fix it.”
4. **Stonewalling** — withdrawing, shutting down, going silent (more common when physiologically flooded).
   - **Antidote: Self-Soothing.** Name the flooding and request a 20-minute break; return and re-engage. A break is not an exit.

## Concrete operational principle (distilled)

**Contempt is the priority.** Among the four, contempt is the only one whose presence (population-level) predicts deterioration even when the others are present. Reducing contempt first — through appreciation rituals and removing contemptuous language — yields the largest improvement.

## How the skill applies this

- The **Four Horsemen Self-Check** (`references/assessments/four-horsemen-self-check.md`) has each partner rate their OWN behavior on the four patterns (1 = never, 5 = almost always). Lower is healthier.
- The skill refuses to use this framework to label a partner (e.g., “my partner is a narcissist who uses contempt”) and instead frames it as mutual habits to address.
- The skill recommends the specific antidote exercise for each partner’s most frequent horseman.

## Effect sizes / evidence quality

- Population-level divorce-prediction accuracy ~0.80 in the presence of frequent horsemen.
- Evidence grade: **A** (strong, population-level).

## Limitations
- The framework was developed observationally; self-report versions are approximations.
- Do not interpret a high self-check score as a prognosis for an individual couple.

## Referral trigger
If a partner’s contempt self-rating is high (4–5) and does not decrease with deliberate practice, recommend couples counseling (contempt is the hardest to reduce alone).
