# Relationship Satisfaction Self-Reflection Prompts (Gottman Sound Relationship House)

> Mutual use: each partner reflects separately, then compare. Not an individual diagnostic.

## The Sound Relationship House Self-Check (administer)

Rate 1–5 (1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree):

1. **(Love Maps)** I can describe my partner’s inner world, stresses, joys, and current dreams.
2. **(Fondness/Admiration)** I express fondness and admiration for my partner regularly.
3. **(Turning Toward)** I turn toward my partner’s small bids for connection.
4. **(Conflict: Gentle Startup)** In a conflict, I start conversations gently rather than with criticism.
5. **(Repair)** We repair effectively when conversations get off track.
6. **(Dreams)** We support each other’s life dreams and roles.
7. **(Shared Meaning)** We share rituals of connection and a sense of shared meaning/values.

Send responses as `srh1=4,srh2=3,srh3=5,srh4=4,srh5=3,srh6=4,srh7=4` to score.

## Conversation prompts by level

### Love Maps
- Who at work is stressing my partner most right now?
- What is my partner’s biggest worry this month?
- Name two of my partner’s friends and how my partner feels about each.
- What is one dream my partner is working toward?

### Fondness & Admiration
- Three specific things my partner did this week that I appreciated.
- One trait I admire in my partner that I have not told them about lately.

### Turning Toward
- One small bid my partner made today that I noticed (a question, a gesture, a story).
- One bid I missed this week that I can circle back to.

### Shared Meaning
- What do we want our home to feel like?
- One ritual we already have; one ritual we want to create.

## Reframe (when the user asks “are we okay / how is our relationship”)

Lead with: “This is a snapshot of how each of you experiences the relationship right now — not a verdict. Let’s look at the seven levels and pick one to strengthen.”
